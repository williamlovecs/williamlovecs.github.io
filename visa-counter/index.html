Visa Quest: Window 7

An unofficial 3D US visa interview adventure. Play four fictional story chapters or practice your own honest English answers for F-1, B1/B2 tourism, family visit, and B-1 business scenarios.

How to play

Choose Story Challenge or Real Practice. Read the brief, walk to Window 7, answer six main questions and any follow-ups, then review clear practice hints. Text input works without a microphone or WebGL.

Practice with clear boundaries

This free game has no login, no live AI, and no visa outcome prediction. Answers stay in memory. Chapter progress and anonymous local events remain on your browser. It is not affiliated with the US government.

Frequently asked questions

Can this predict my visa result? No. It only supports language practice and cannot assess eligibility, documents, honesty, or approval probability.

Do I need a microphone? No. Every chapter can be completed with text input.
